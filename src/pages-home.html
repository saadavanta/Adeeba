About Adeeba Asif

A thoughtful, family-centred approach to nutrition.

“I don’t want to simply tell people what to eat. I want to help them understand why.”

Read the full introduction

My journey into nutrition began with a simple question: Why? From a young age, I often heard people recommend certain foods for fever, colds, or illness but rarely explain why they were recommended, when they should be eaten, or how much was appropriate. That curiosity led me to study Nutrition & Dietetics — not simply to learn what to eat, but to understand the science behind food, health and everyday choices.

My work focuses on Pediatric Nutrition and Behavioral & Psychological Nutrition, with particular attention to helping parents understand the reasons behind their child’s eating behavior rather than simply labelling them a “picky eater.”
